Batch: many students

One student per line, columns separated by commas (a CSV file works too, and its heading row is skipped). You get one PDF with a cover page for every student (up to 100).

{1} is the first column, {2} the second, and so on. A line is left out if its column is empty. Everything else (college, subject, design) comes from the form above.
